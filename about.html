WHERE I AM NOW

Building a world
one system at a time.

Matt's Pocket Farm is an independent browser game shaped around a single explorable island. Crops, animal care, supplies and rewards work together as one playful loop.

I built the landscape with code, drew the 3D scene on canvas, and designed every interaction for mouse, touch and keyboard. It shows how I think: start simple, notice the details and keep improving.

“A small world becomes memorable when every click changes something.”
