## Title

Matt's Pocket Farm

## Short description

An interactive 3D personal farm where visitors explore projects, plant crops and meet the maker.

## Long description

Matt's Pocket Farm is my independent single-player farming game presented as a small digital world. Players rotate the island through a fixed 360-degree view and choose the supply house, crop field, animal meadow or fish pond.

The game is for casual players who enjoy short farming and management loops. The supply house stores seeds, animal feed and fish bait; the other areas turn those supplies into crops, milk, chicken meat and fish meat that can be sold for coins.

I built the complete scene with semantic HTML, responsive CSS, plain JavaScript and the Canvas API. The buildings, landscape, crops and camera system are generated with code rather than downloaded 3D models. Dragging changes the camera angle, scrolling changes the zoom, and clicking places opens interactive panels without leaving the main world.

The farm game uses state changes, immediate visual feedback and browser storage. Players spend coins, care for crops and animals, collect products, sell them and reinvest the income. The site also includes optional sound and responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
